          The pipeline

          Everyone’s surplus, sorted by machine.

          Live intake · 38 stores connected · parcels move to Rescue as each reject list updates.

          Supermarkets already file a rejected-goods list every day — for audit, for write-off, for insurance. We plug into those feeds. A model reads every line, scores how sellable it is against our own health-and-hygiene rules, and routes it. People spot-check; the machine does the first pass on all of it.

          Concept figures. “Sky&More” and “Your Neighbour Grocery” stand in for the long tail of small chains and corner shops that a manual buyer could never work through.

          1. Ingest

            We read each partner’s daily reject list straight from their system: product, quantity, stated reason, batch, best-before, and the cold-storage temperature log.

            Live feeds: 38

          2. AI triage

            A model scores every line for sellability — shelf-life left, why it was pulled, storage history, recall status — and sorts roughly 1,900 lines a day in minutes.

            Avg score: 7.4 / 10

          3. Our standards

            Then the hard rules: cold chain never broken, a safe date margin, packaging intact, allergens labelled, nothing under recall.

            Pass rate: 71%

            → Fails go to compost, animal feed, or back to the store.

          4. On the shelf

            What passes is priced — about a third under retail, usually more — and listed on the shop tab, most of it within the hour.

            Median time in: 52 min
